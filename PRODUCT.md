# VSTMailer
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Approved: Next.js, TypeScript, Fastify, PostgreSQL, self-hosted Postal and portable Linux deployment.
## Users
Businesses and developers sending transactional email and permission-based campaigns; platform operators managing delivery and abuse.
## Product Purpose
An independently operated mailing platform with monthly subscription allowances and support for people without domains.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Tenant isolation, durable send acceptance, quota accounting, hosted send-only identities, verified custom domains. Personal Gmail/Outlook inboxes are signup and Reply-To addresses; actual provider-account sending and full hosted mailboxes are out of initial scope. Millions-per-month capacity is a qualification target, not a verified claim.

Business registration: Nigeria. Billing currency: USD, explicitly confirmed by the owner. Plan prices and payment-provider credentials are not yet supplied. Never silently convert prices to NGN or claim live billing is enabled.
## Brand Commitments
VSTMailer. VST is short for Velion Smart Technologies. Use the original logo supplied by the user, its emerald tones and dark navy identity. Brand source: apps/web/public/velion-brand.png, preserved unmodified.
## Evidence on Hand
User-approved design in docs/design.md and the supplied logo. No live customer metrics, testimonials, commercial prices or production capacity evidence exist.
## Product Principles
Own delivery infrastructure. Account for every recipient. Show truthful delivery states. Make failures actionable. Keep customer data separate.
